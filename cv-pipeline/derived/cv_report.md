## Inter-assay CV -- standards-based (primary for cytokines/CRP, see methodology note)
Cytokine/CRP inter-assay CV is computed from Standard1-6 Avg Result across plates rather than repeated controls, because this dataset has essentially no repeated control data (only 1 of 8 cytokine plates has control wells; CRP has none at all). Standards are an established substrate for inter-assay CV in the immunoassay literature (Hanneman et al., 2011, Biological Research for Nursing 13(3):243-250; Grotjan & Keel, 1996), which found no significant difference between CVs computed from controls, standards, or unknowns. The literature's caveat about once-per-box-reconstituted standards inflating cross-plate agreement does not apply here: every plate in this dataset, including same-day pairs, used a distinctly labeled kit box. Treated as the primary inter-assay CV for cytokines/CRP.
